Potential Resources Available to Address Significant Health Needs  
Understanding the potential resources to address significant health needs is fundamental to 
determining current state capacity and gaps. The organized health care delivery systems include the 
Department of Public Health Providence Valdez Medical Center. In addition, there are numerous social 
service non-profit agencies, faith-based organizations, and school district that contribute resources to 
address these identified needs. For a list of potentially available resources available to address 
significant health needs see Appendix 2.
