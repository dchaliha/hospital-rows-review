Valdez Food Bank’s mission is to assist people in need and lacking sufficient nutrition through regularly 
scheduled distribution of basic food items. Our clients are underemployed, on disability, unemployed, or 
experiencing circumstances beyond control. The Valdez Food Bank also provides other types of 
emergency assistance such as heating, electricity, dental services, medical prescriptions and treatment. 
Such assistance is awarded on a case by case basis by the board and requires evidence of actual dire 
need. This assistance is given in the absence of other available sources.
