Summary of Community Needs Assessment Process and Results 
In gathering information on the communities served by the Providence Valdez Medical Center we 
looked not only at the health conditions of the population, but also at socioeconomic factors, the 
physical environment, and health behaviors. In addition, we recognize that there are often geographic 
areas where the conditions for supporting health are poorer than nearby areas. Whenever possible and 
reliable, data are reported at census tract level. These smaller geographic areas allow us to better 
understand the neighborhood level needs of our communities and better address inequities within and 
across communities.
